Our mission

Technology for Every Learner

I am Divine Mutesi, and my mission is to bring technology into education, especially in rural areas. StoryCast is where I share what I learn on my visits, in formats everyone can use: audio, video, captions and text.

Accessibility features

How the site meets WCAG 2.1 AA

  • Full transcripts

    Every story has a complete transcript on its page and a downloadable plain-text copy.

  • Captions

    Video uses the track element with a WebVTT captions file, switched on by default.

  • Keyboard navigation

    Every control is reachable by keyboard, with a skip link and a clear orange focus ring.

  • Colour contrast

    Text and controls meet the WCAG 2.1 AA ratio of 4.5:1 or better.

  • Screen reader support

    Semantic landmarks, one h1 per page, descriptive alt text and labelled media.

  • Responsive layout

    Grid, Flexbox and a container-queried card adapt to any screen size.

About the creator

Who I am

I want every child to have the chance to learn with technology, wherever they live. I visit rural communities to see how people already learn and where technology could help most.

My approach

I listen first, then share. Each story is paired with a transcript and captions, and I check every page with a keyboard, a contrast calculator and heading-order review.

How to engage

Ways to experience StoryCast

  1. Browse stories on the Home page.
  2. Open a story to hear the audio and watch the captioned video.
  3. Read the transcript below the player to follow without sound.
  4. Download the plain-text transcript for offline reading.
  5. Use the player controls to turn captions on or off.
